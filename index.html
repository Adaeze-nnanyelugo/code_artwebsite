A little about the artist

More Than a Portrait. A Story Preserved.

I am a portrait artist based in Nigeria, creating expressive artworks that celebrate individuality, identity, emotion, and the beauty of the human experience.

My work is inspired by the subtle details that make every face unique: the depth in a person's eyes, the quiet confidence in their expression, the stories carried in their features, and the emotions that words sometimes cannot explain.

For me, portraiture is more than recreating a likeness. It is about capturing a presence. Through careful observation, patience, and attention to detail, I transform photographs and personal references into meaningful works of art that people can treasure for years.

Whether I am creating a personal keepsake, a thoughtful gift, a family portrait, or a statement piece for a collection, my goal remains the same: to create art that feels personal, timeless, and deeply human.

Every commission is approached with care, from understanding the client's vision to refining the final details. I believe the best artwork does more than decorate a space; it creates a connection between the viewer and the story behind it.

How do I commission a portrait?

Complete the commission inquiry form with your preferred medium, size, budget, reference photograph, and desired deadline. Your request is an inquiry, not a confirmed order. Scope, pricing, availability, and next steps will need to be agreed.

What reference photographs should I provide?

As a starting point, choose a clear, well-lit photograph with visible facial features and the expression you want to preserve. Share only photographs you have permission to provide. The artist can confirm whether a reference is suitable after reviewing it securely.

How much does a custom portrait cost?

Pricing may depend on size, medium, number of subjects, complexity, and delivery requirements. Any prices shown in this demonstration are illustrative only. Insert your confirmed price guide or request an individual quote.

How long does a portrait take?

Completion time depends on the project and current workload. Share your desired deadline in the form. Do not treat it as accepted until the artist confirms a feasible schedule.

Can I request changes to my artwork?

Revision availability and scope should be agreed before the project begins. Add your actual revision policy here, including any limits or additional costs, if applicable.

Do you offer digital portraits?

Digital portraits are included as a proposed service on this website. Confirm current availability, file format, resolution, delivery method, permitted uses, and pricing with the artist.

Can I order a portrait as a gift?

You can submit an inquiry for a gift portrait and include the occasion and desired deadline. Please allow time for the artist to confirm whether the request can be completed.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

International shipping availability, cost, packaging, customs charges, and delivery times have not been confirmed here. Contact the studio before placing an order, and add your verified shipping policy to this answer.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment methods have not been configured on this demonstration site. Insert your confirmed payment methods and payment schedule. Secure online payment requires a properly configured payment provider and backend.

What is your cancellation or refund policy?

Insert your actual cancellation, deposit, refund, and return policy here. Explain how custom work and digital products are handled, and obtain appropriate professional guidance for the rules that apply to your business.

Can I use my portrait for commercial purposes?

Do not assume a personal commission includes commercial usage rights. Intended use, reproduction, licensing, and copyright arrangements should be discussed and agreed in writing before work begins.
